## Interpretation
- **Revenue almost doubled (+93%)** — AI data-centre demand kept compounding through 2026.
- **Profit grew faster than revenue** (PAT +128%): gross margin recovered from the H20-hit
  2025 base (70.0% → 74.9%) and operating leverage improved as expenses grew slower than sales.
- **Other Income is the swing factor:** +416%, driven by equity-securities mark-to-market gains
  (notably ~$15,936M in Q1 FY2027). Excluding such gains, operating income growth (+117%) is
  the cleaner measure of business performance.
- **Costs are controlled:** R&D grew +58% and SG&A only +32% against +93% revenue growth.
- **Caution:** CY2026 currently covers the four quarters ended Oct 2026; the Nov–Dec 2026
  quarter (Q4 FY2027, guided revenue ≈ $130,000M ± 3%) will raise every CY2026 figure further.
