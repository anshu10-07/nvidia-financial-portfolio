*(All figures in USD millions, except EPS and percentages. GAAP basis.)*

> **Period note:** NVIDIA reports on a fiscal year ending in late January, not calendar quarters.
> Calendar-year figures are built by adding the four reported fiscal quarters that fall in each
> calendar block — CY2025 = Q4 FY2025 + Q1–Q3 FY2026 (quarters ended Jan 2025 – Oct 2025);
> CY2026 = Q4 FY2026 + Q1–Q3 FY2027 (quarters ended Jan 2026 – Oct 2026). Both periods are
> 12 months (4 quarters), so the comparison is like-for-like. Q4 CY2026 (Nov–Dec 2026) is not
> yet reported; NVIDIA has guided Q4 FY2027 revenue of about $130,000M ± 3%.

## Notes to the P&L Account
1. **H20 charge (CY2025):** Q1 FY2026 cost of revenue includes a $4,500M charge on H20 products
   (China export restrictions), which pulled that quarter's gross margin to 60.5%. Q2 FY2026
   includes a $180M release of previously reserved H20 inventory.
2. **Equity-securities gains (inside Other Income, GAAP only):** CY2025 ≈ $9,034M across the
   block (Q2 FY26 $2,247M; Q3 FY26 $1,354M; Q4 FY26 $5,491M — see quarterly file); CY2026 includes
   very large mark-to-market gains — Q1 FY2027 alone $15,936M and Q2 FY2027 $7,771M. These are
   the main reason "Other Income" jumps 416% in CY2026 and why GAAP profit grows faster than
   operating performance alone.
3. **H200 charge (CY2026):** a $400M H200-related charge sits in H1 FY2027 cost of revenue
   (quarter split not disclosed).
4. Q3 FY2027 operating expenses include a $107M legal settlement and $208M acquisition-related costs.
5. Figures are as reported in millions; $1M rounding differences exist on some interest/other
   lines between NVIDIA's releases and aggregators — one consistent basis (official releases)
   is used throughout, and every line cross-foots (Revenue − Cost = Gross Profit, etc.).
6. Q3 FY2027 interest income/expense split was not yet published (10-Q pending at 18 Nov 2026),
   so Other Income is shown as one net line, exactly as NVIDIA's Q2–Q3 FY2027 statements present it.
