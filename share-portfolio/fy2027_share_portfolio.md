## 3. Current affairs — the CEO resignation and its market effect

### What happened (official record)
- On **11 Nov 2026** NVIDIA's board **accepted Jen-Hsun (Jensen) Huang's resignation as President, CEO and board member, effective immediately**. It was **publicly announced on 12 Nov 2026** (company press release; SEC Form 8-K).
- **Colette M. Kress** (CFO since 2013) became **interim CEO** while continuing as CFO. **Harvey C. Jones** (director since 1993) became the company's first-ever **Chair of the Board**. A search for a permanent CEO (internal and external candidates) is under way; **no permanent successor had been named as of 18 Nov 2026**.
- The official filing and release state **no reason** for the resignation, and Huang took **no new role** — he left the board entirely.
- *Press-reported context (allegations, not established findings):* reports in the days before the announcement described a board investigation and personal-conduct allegations. The board stated it had "no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised"; the investigation has published no findings. On the 17 Nov earnings call, Chair Jones ruled out reinstating Huang.

### Analyst reaction (12–18 Nov 2026)
A wave of downgrades and target cuts followed the announcement — Bernstein cut its target $400 → $200 (downgrade to Market Perform), Evercore $465 → $186, HSBC $365 → $170 (to Hold), JPMorgan $320 → $215, Barclays $275 → $210, UBS → $240, D.A. Davidson $300 → $200 (Buy kept), among others. After the 17 Nov earnings, Jefferies trimmed $300 → $250 and Morgan Stanley $267 → $260; Summit Insights downgraded to Hold.

### Earnings steadied the stock (17 Nov 2026)
Q3 FY2027 results (released after the close on 17 Nov) beat expectations — revenue $115.8bn (+103% YoY) vs ~$109bn consensus, non-GAAP EPS $2.68 vs $2.47 expected, Q4 guidance $130bn ± 3% — and the shares rose **+4.81% after hours to $162.94**. Interim CEO Kress told analysts it was "business as usual", raised the FY2028 growth outlook from 70% to 75%, and the board created a national-security/export-control oversight committee. (No regular-session close for 18 Nov exists yet.)

## 4. Other FY2027 events that moved the shares
- **China / export controls (through 2026):** US moved to case-by-case H200 licensing in Jan 2026, China restricted sales, and NVIDIA took a **$0.4bn H200 charge in H1 FY2027**; all 2026 guidance assumes **zero China data-centre compute revenue**.
- **Q1 FY2027 earnings (20 May 2026):** revenue $81.6bn; stock −1.8% the next day.
- **~$25bn bond issue (15–16 Jun 2026):** $24,896M across 7 tranches (first bond sale since 2021, ~$85bn of orders); no distinct share-price reaction identified.
- **27 Jul 2026:** stock −4.99% to $196.51 amid SK Group partnership LOIs (>$500bn) and reported ~$250bn OpenAI backstop talks.
- **Q2 FY2027 earnings (26 Aug 2026):** revenue $96.2bn; stock **+8.74% to $227.98** the next day — about +$441.5bn of market cap in one session.
- **6 Oct 2026:** all-time peak ($243.37 intraday; $5.78tn market cap), followed by six straight weekly losses and a bear-market entry on 5 Nov (−9.26%).
- **10 Nov 2026:** Anthropic's S-1 filing showed NVIDIA to buy ~$10bn of shares at the IPO price.

## 5. Reading of the portfolio
FY2027 splits into two halves: a strong operating run (record revenue every quarter, dividend up 25×, $230bn of new buyback authorisations, all-time high in October) and a governance shock in November that — layered on a correction already under way — cut the market value by roughly a fifth from the fiscal year's start. The 17 Nov earnings reaction (+4.81% after hours) suggests investors are separating the leadership crisis from the business itself, but with no permanent CEO named and analyst targets cut sharply, the shares remain well below their October peak.
