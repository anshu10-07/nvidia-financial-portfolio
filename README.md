## Method
NVIDIA's fiscal year ends in late January, so calendar-year (Jan–Dec) figures are the sum of the
four reported fiscal quarters in each calendar block: CY2025 = Q4 FY2025 + Q1–Q3 FY2026;
CY2026 = Q4 FY2026 + Q1–Q3 FY2027 (the latest quarter, Q3 FY2027, was reported 17 Nov 2026).
Both periods are 12 months, so the comparison is like-for-like. CY2026's final quarter
(Nov–Dec 2026) is not yet reported — NVIDIA guided Q4 FY2027 revenue of ~$130,000M ± 3%.

All figures are GAAP, in USD millions, taken from NVIDIA's earnings releases / SEC filings
(see `data/sources.md`) and cross-checked line-by-line; every total cross-foots.
